# Governance

Richard M. Hamilton (@rhamenator) maintains ViVe Desktop. This is presently a
solo-maintainer project. A required PR and passing automated checks are not a
claim of independent human review; the maintainer may self-review and merge
their own changes after the gates pass.

The default branch requires passing Desktop, Store-package, repository-policy
and CodeQL checks, an up-to-date branch and resolved review conversations.
Force pushes and deletion are disabled. Release tag history is protected.
Dependencies and Actions are monitored by Dependabot; security reports should
use private vulnerability reporting.

The app is offered free. Optional donations do not purchase a feature, service,
license exception, priority support or any other consideration. GPL rights and
third-party attribution remain unchanged.
